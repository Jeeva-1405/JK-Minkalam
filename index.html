Tell us what’s going wrong.

Most of our projects start with a problem, not a product code. Here are the six we hear most, and how each one ends.

  1. 01

    Power cuts stop our production.

    Fixed withpower backup & power quality.

    Online UPS sized to your real load, industrial battery banks for the backup time you need, and stabilisers, isolation transformers and surge protection so the supply that reaches your machines is clean.

    Exide, Numax, Schneider Electric, Eaton

    Talk to an engineer
  2. 02

    Our electricity bill keeps climbing.

    Fixed withenergy management & metering.

    Measure first. Multifunction meters and power-quality analysers show where the energy goes and where it’s wasted, then an energy management system keeps tracking it, feeder by feeder.

    Rishabh, Schneider Electric

    Talk to an engineer
  3. 03

    The server room runs hot and full.

    Fixed witha data centre that watches itself.

    Rittal racks and containment, precision cooling and intelligent PDUs, from a single edge cabinet to a full server room. Temperature, humidity and differential-pressure sensors warn you before anything overheats.

    Rittal, HP, Lenovo, Rishabh

    Talk to an engineer
  4. 04

    We can’t see what the plant is doing.

    Fixed withSCADA & IIoT.

    Rishabh PROMOTIC SCADA gives you live dashboards, alarms, a historian and automated reports. IIoT edge gateways connect the machines SCADA can’t reach and track OEE from anywhere.

    Rishabh PROMOTIC, MELSS, Utthunga

    Talk to an engineer
  5. 05

    Machines need constant babysitting.

    Fixed withPLC automation.

    Schneider Modicon, Siemens SIMATIC and Allen-Bradley PLCs, programmed and integrated with HMI, VFDs and MCC panels. Add predictive maintenance so breakdowns are caught before they stop the line.

    Schneider Electric, Siemens, Rockwell Automation, MELSS

    Talk to an engineer
  6. 06

    We need eyes on the site, day and night.

    Fixed withCCTV & gas detection.

    HiFocus IP and PTZ cameras with NVRs, perimeter security and AI video analytics, viewable remotely. For hazardous areas, gas, VOC and PM2.5/PM10 monitoring tied into your alarms.

    HiFocus, Rishabh

    Talk to an engineer
